## Categories searched but NOT delivered

### 6. `alarm.mp3` — general quarters / ship alarm — **NOT FOUND (verified clean recording)**
Three carrier GQ-drill B-roll videos were downloaded and spectrum-analysed; none contains an
isolable alarm tone (tonal concentration never exceeded ~2% — the alarm is either not sounded
during filming or is buried under hangar/deck noise):
- Video 995497, `DOD_111517444` — "Abraham Lincoln conducts general quarters drill." (SA Cesar
  Zavala Cordova, USS Abraham Lincoln, Dec 2025) — PUBLIC DOMAIN page, no usable alarm.
- Video 995489, `DOD_111517436` — "Abraham Lincoln conducts a general quarters drill" (PO3 Glory
  Anderson, Jan 2026) — uniform ambient level, no alarm event.
- Video 900911, `DOD_109947418` — "IKE Conducts a General Quarters Drill" (PO2 Ray McCann, USS
  Dwight D. Eisenhower, Oct 2023) — starts in silence, drill activity only.
Recommendation: keep the game's synthesized alarm, or source a klaxon from a non-DVIDS
public-domain effects library with its own license check.

### 7. `radio-chatter.mp3` — real military radio chatter — **NOT FOUND on DVIDS**
DVIDS carries no standalone tactical radio-chatter audio asset. Search results were limited to
narrated AFN news packages (e.g. an MQ-9 "radio control tower" package, Audio by Sgt. Robert
Liddy — narration, not usable chatter) and LSO interview/production videos. Real air-traffic /
LSO radio traffic is not published as audio on DVIDS. Recommendation: keep the produced voice
clips + radio FX chain already in the game; do not substitute narrated packages.

## Method / verification notes
- Direct downloads from DVIDS' CloudFront CDN (`d34w7g4gy10iej.cloudfront.net`) succeeded from
  the VM for both audio MP3s and video MP4s; DVIDS' own site search endpoint returns a bot
  interstitial (HTTP 202, empty) to curl, so discovery was done via web search of the DVIDS
  index, then asset pages were fetched directly.
- Every delivered file was verified after production: `file` reports MPEG layer III audio and
  `ffprobe` confirms the durations listed above.
- A narrated newscast ("Dyess Test Call", Audio ID 33528) was probed early and **rejected**:
  DVIDS audio assets are mostly narrated packages, unsuitable as clean sound effects — which is
  why all delivered recordings come from natural-sound B-roll video audio tracks instead.
